## Deviations and findings

- **Outputs live in `src/generated/`, not `dist/` (D-23).** The root `.gitignore` ignores every `dist`, and TypeScript only compiles under `src/`. Spec, runbook and ADR text were updated.
- **DTCG 2025.10 support in Style Dictionary 5.7.1 is enough for our subset.** Aliases, object-form dimensions and durations, shadows, font families, font weights, numbers, cubic béziers and colours with alpha all work. The partial support reported in ARCH-001 §6.5 did not affect us, because our own formats serialise every value.
- **`name/kebab` transform needed.** Without it, Style Dictionary names tokens by their last path segment and reports light/dark collisions.
- **Atomic build.** The first version deleted `src/generated` before rebuilding. Typecheck and tests running in parallel could then see the files missing. The build now writes to a temp folder and copies only changed files.
- **Prettier ignores `pnpm-lock.yaml`.** Adding a dependency produced a lockfile Prettier would reformat; the lockfile belongs to pnpm.
- **Default palette.** The semantic defaults are the neutral embedded theme from `SPECS/validation/tenant-fixtures.md` (primary and accent `#334155`). Tenant colours arrive through the theme contract in P1.3.
- **Extra colour roles** beyond THEME-02's 13: `surface-container`, `on-primary-container`, `outline-variant` and `backdrop`. They are platform-defined, not tenant-editable.

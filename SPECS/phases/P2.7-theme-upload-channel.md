## 1. Goal

Tenant admins (for their own theme) and platform admins upload a design system's DTCG files from the dashboard, build or confirm its manifest in a mapping step, and get a pending theme version produced by the same generator as repository themes. Same source, same artifact, whichever channel.

## 2. Preconditions

1. `STATUS.md`: P2.6 `Accepted`, P2.7 `Approved`.
2. P2.2 and P2.3 revised to produce a theme source and manifest instead of a `theme.v1` projection, and approved.

## 3. Scope

**In:** upload route behind `requireTenantTarget`; intake (several files, folder, ZIP) and limits from EXTRACT-01/02; storage in `theme_sources`; mapping step from PROJ-05 producing `semanticMap`; server-side generation; lossy-conversion approval; the editor's publish creating a `theme-v1` version of the tenant's own theme.

**Out:** CSS and Tailwind input (EXTRACT-05A, later); legacy formats (EXTRACT-05, later).

## 4. Decisions applied

D-30. Fixed here: uploads never accept CSS, SCSS or other generated code; each uploaded theme has `source_origin = upload`; sources are kept byte for byte.

## 7. Quality gates introduced

None new; uploads pass G1–G11.

## 8. Verification procedure (agent)

1. Dashboard unit tests and `pnpm nx e2e @byteflock/admin-dashboard-e2e -- --project=chromium --grep "theme upload"` → green.
2. `pnpm verify --quick` → green.

## 9. Manual validation (human)

Upload one real export as a tenant admin; map, approve, publish.

## 10. Evidence to record

Equivalence results; limits; screenshots; deviations.

## 11. Exit and approval

P2.7 → `Accepted` by the product owner. P2.2 and P2.3 are then closed as superseded.
